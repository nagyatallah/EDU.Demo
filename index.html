About us

Cyprus Gateway Education connects ambitious students with English-taught university programs across North Cyprus. Our advisors have walked this path themselves — from choosing a university to settling into a new city — and we use that experience to make yours simpler.

  • Partner universities across Nicosia, Famagusta, and Kyrenia
  • Application and visa support from start to finish
  • Airport pickup and housing assistance on arrival
  • Advisors who speak English, Arabic, and Turkish
